About Me

Engineering strategy grounded in delivery.

I’m a Staff Software Engineer with 16 years of experience building web applications, internal platforms, AI-powered experiences, and interactive advertising products. My work includes LLM-driven products for BuzzFeed, custom tooling for campaign teams, and publisher platforms supporting more than 100 partner websites.
