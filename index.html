How I create leverage

Staff impact through systems, not just launches.

I work where product definition meets frontend architecture. That often means taking a loosely defined request, finding the durable technical model inside it, and aligning product, engineering, and operations around something teams can build and maintain.

At BuzzFeed, that looked like connecting LLM output, editor state, live previews, and canvas rendering through shared data contracts. It also meant reshaping campaign tooling around the real workflow used by engineering, RevTech, and AdOps—and making the case for dedicated infrastructure when the existing stack imposed limits.

Earlier, at Complex Networks, I modernized a publisher platform deployed across more than 100 partner sites and co-architected the tooling used to manage and ship rich-media campaigns. The thread across the work is the same: make complexity explicit, build the reusable layer, and keep delivery moving.

What I bring to a team

  1. 01

    Turn ambiguity into a buildable model

    Define requirements, constraints, ownership, and the data contract before complexity spreads through the interface.

  2. 02

    Find the platform inside the launch

    Extract shared builders, renderers, components, and configuration so the next format does not start from zero.

  3. 03

    Work across the delivery boundary

    Partner with product, editorial, revenue, and operations teams to make the technical system fit how work actually ships.

01

AI creation platform · BuzzFeed

One data contract from prompt to editable canvas

Problem

AI experiences were becoming a family of products: user input had to become structured model output, flow into a live preview, and remain editable inside a visual builder.

My contribution

Led core engineering for Generator Generator and BF Island Builders. Designed the relationship among JSON configuration, Mustache variables, LLM output, editor state, shared APIs, renderers, and a Fabric.js canvas layout block.

Leverage created

Refactored the approach toward shared components and configuration-driven formats, establishing a reusable path for new builders instead of treating each experience as a standalone implementation.
